Privacy Policy: Masamune

Developer: BurnsChronik Games · Contact: burnschronik@gmail.com
Last updated: October 28, 2026

Masamune ("the game", Android package com.burnschronik.masamune) is made by burnschronik games ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with your information.

The short version

The game does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

The game saves your settings on your device: graphics quality, touch controls, haptics, volume, the Reduce flashing option, and whether you have finished the training court. This file never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage, deletes it.

Permissions

The game asks for one Android permission, vibrate, for haptic feedback during play. You can turn haptics off in the game's Settings.

Google Play

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google handles downloads, installs and crash reports under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). From Google Play Console we see only aggregated, anonymous statistics, such as install counts and crash rates. They do not identify you.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Since it collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
