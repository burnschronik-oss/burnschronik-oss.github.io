Privacy Policy: Latent: Phase Merge

Developer: BurnsChronik Games · Contact: burnschronik@gmail.com
Effective date: October 13, 2026

This policy explains how Latent: Phase Merge (Android package com.burnschronik.latent) handles information.

No account; no personal information collected by the developer

The game does not require an account. The developer does not collect, store or sell your name, email or other personal information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, and usage and diagnostic data to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent (EEA, UK)

In the European Economic Area and the UK, the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can choose non-personalized ads, and change your choice from the game's privacy settings where offered.

In-app purchase

An optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. The developer does not receive your payment details; Google Play's terms and privacy policy apply.

Data stored on your device

Basic gameplay data such as progress, settings and purchase status is stored locally on your device and is not sent to the developer.

Audience and children

Latent: Phase Merge is designed for a general audience, including kids. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where required (for example for users identified as children, or where consent is not given), ads are requested as non-personalized and with family-safe content settings through AdMob. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the game to remove its locally stored data. To reset or delete your advertising ID, go to Android Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Google > Ads).

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
