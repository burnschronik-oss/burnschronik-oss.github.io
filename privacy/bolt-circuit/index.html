Privacy Policy: BOLT CIRCUIT

Developer: BurnsChronik Games · Contact: burnschronik@gmail.com
Effective date: October 30, 2026

This policy explains how BOLT CIRCUIT handles information.

No personal data collected

BOLT CIRCUIT does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The game does not require an account, and the developer receives no data from it.

No ads, no purchases

This version of the game shows no advertising, contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and has no in-app purchases.

Works offline

The game does not request internet access and plays fully offline. Its only device permission is vibration, used for haptic feedback.

Data stored on your device

Gameplay progress and settings are saved only on your device and are never sent to the developer or anyone else.

Children

BOLT CIRCUIT is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the game to remove its locally stored data.

Changes

If a future update adds advertising, in-app purchases or any other feature that handles data, this policy will be updated before that update is released, and the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions or requests: burnschronik@gmail.com
