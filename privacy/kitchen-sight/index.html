Privacy Policy: Kitchen Sight: Pantry Meals

Developer: BurnsChronik Games · Contact: burnschronik@gmail.com
Effective date: October 13, 2026

This policy explains how Kitchen Sight: Pantry Meals (Android package app.kitchensight.pantry) handles information.

Summary

Kitchen Sight is an offline, local-first app. There is no account, no sign-in and no advertising. The developer does not collect, receive or sell your personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your pantry items, recipes, preferences and other app data are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded to the developer.

Camera

The app uses the camera, with your permission, to scan barcodes and food labels. Scanning is processed on the device. Camera images are not sent to the developer.

Barcode lookups (Open Food Facts)

When you look up a scanned barcode, the app sends the barcode number to the Open Food Facts database to get product details. Like any web request, this exposes your IP address to Open Food Facts. Their handling is described in the Open Food Facts privacy policy. No account or personal details are sent.

Ads and purchases

The app shows no ads and contains no advertising SDKs.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app, to remove all data stored by the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions or requests: burnschronik@gmail.com
