OVERLAND: Off-Road Rally

Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026

OVERLAND: Off-Road Rally ("the app") is made by BurnsChronik Games. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Summary

OVERLAND: Off-Road Rally is free to play and shows ads served by Google AdMob. An optional one-time in-app purchase removes ads. The app has no accounts or sign-in, and BurnsChronik Games does not itself collect, store or sell your personal information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to show rewarded and interstitial ads. AdMob may automatically collect and share with Google the following data: device or other identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, ad interactions (such as ad views and taps), and diagnostics (such as crash logs and performance data). Google uses this data to serve and measure ads, for analytics, and to prevent fraud.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and certain US states), the app asks for your consent using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the app's privacy settings when available.

Opting out of personalized ads

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device settings (on most Android devices: Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we do not receive or store your payment details. The app receives only a confirmation of the purchase so it can turn off ads, and Google keeps a purchase history under its own policies.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, settings, best times or scores, and your ad-free status are saved on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
